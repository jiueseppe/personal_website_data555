Autism Insurance Mandates and Behavioral Treatment Dashboard

This interactive Shiny dashboard explores how state autism insurance mandate generosity varies across states and years, and how those policy differences relate to behavioral treatment utilization among privately insured children with autism.

The dashboard was developed as part of my graduate data science and thesis work. It combines a state-year autism insurance mandate policy file with an aggregated National Survey of Children’s Health summary dataset, allowing users to compare policy generosity, behavioral treatment percentages, and age-group patterns from 2016 to 2023.

Graduate Thesis: Autism Insurance Mandate Generosity and Behavioral Treatment Use in Privately Insured Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder

This graduate thesis manuscript examines whether more generous state autism insurance mandates are associated with greater behavioral treatment use among privately insured children with autism. The project focuses on how specific policy design features, including age limits and dollar caps, may shape access to autism-related services.

Using pooled 2016–2023 National Survey of Children’s Health data, I analyzed the relationship between state mandate generosity and behavioral treatment utilization while accounting for child, family, and state-level factors. This work reflects my broader interest in health policy, disability access, and evidence-based approaches to improving care for children with developmental disabilities.
